Teeth Whitening

Teeth whitening is a popular cosmetic dental procedure that brightens the color of teeth, addressing stains and discoloration caused by various factors, such as the natural aging process, consumption of staining substances like coffee, tea, and red wine, tobacco use, certain medications, and poor oral hygiene.

Our expert cosmetic dentists use advanced treatments with safe and effective bleaching agents, such as hydrogen peroxide or carbamide peroxide, to break down stains within the tooth structure and restore a brighter, more youthful smile. Visit our teeth whitening clinic in Dubai and Abu Dhabi to experience the transformation today.






Wisdom Tooth Extraction

At Amber Dental Clinics in Dubai and Abu Dhabi, we understand that wisdom teeth can be a source of discomfort and concern. These third molars often erupt later in life and can sometimes cause problems when they don't have enough space to come in properly. If you're experiencing pain, swelling, or other issues related to your wisdom teeth, wisdom tooth extraction may be the recommended course of action.



Dental Fillings

Tooth decay is a common concern, but it doesn't have to compromise your oral health or your smile. At Amber Dental Clinics in Dubai and Abu Dhabi, we provide top-quality dental fillings that not only restore the function of your teeth but also seamlessly blend with your natural smile.

Dental fillings are restorative materials used to repair teeth affected by cavities, fractures, or wear. They are often crafted from different materials such as composite resin, porcelain, glass ionomer, and gold.
